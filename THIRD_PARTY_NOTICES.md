## Specifications

`corpus/` is mostly frozen index and config artifacts that point at upstream
specifications rather than vendoring them. The exception is
`corpus/configs/apalache-annotated/`, which holds annotated derivatives of
specs from the 206-spec FormaLLM corpus; those remain under their upstream
license and are included here only as evidence for the Apalache sweep.

TLA+ specifications under `results/` are generated by this project (the W4
cross-family corpus and per-run model outputs) and are covered by the MIT
license above.
