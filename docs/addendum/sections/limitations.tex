\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

The grammar has not been run inside a generation arm. The 86.7\% catch rate
is an offline measurement over saved candidates, an upper bound on what
decode-time constraint would remove. No claim about pass rate under
constrained decoding is supported by this addendum.

The 2x2 is incomplete. Framing L has been measured on the base model only.
The tuned-model cell requires a self-hosted serve that has not yet run, so
this addendum does not report what task training adds on top of the loop.

Every result in this addendum comes from one model family, one model size
(gpt-oss-120b), and one 30-spec holdout.

Spec-level pass@k at $k{=}32$ is noisy: the open-loop control moved 12, 8,
and 12 across its three seeds while its per-sample SANY rate stayed flat.

The shared inference endpoint used for every arm in this addendum accepts
vLLM's \texttt{guided\_grammar} and \texttt{guided\_choice} parameters with
HTTP 200 and applies neither; this was verified by sending
\texttt{guided\_choice: ["alpha","beta"]} and receiving a free-form sentence
in response. Constrained decoding requires a self-hosted serve.

4 of the 30 holdout specs are library modules scored on SANY alone and 2 are
proof modules scored on TLAPS; 24 of the 30 are graded by TLC.
