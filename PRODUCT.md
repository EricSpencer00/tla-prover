# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Formal-methods researchers, TLA+ practitioners, model-training researchers, and AI4FM collaborators who need to inspect claims, reproduce measurements, run small checks, and understand what has and has not passed without reading an agent transcript.

## Product Purpose

Make the TLA-Prover program legible and runnable as a public research instrument. The site gathers the fixed goals, frozen gates, measured results, active experiment, source artifacts, and a real TLAKit playground in one place. Success means a visitor can distinguish verified evidence from infrastructure status, reproduce a result, and contribute without relying on private context.

## Brand Personality

Exacting, candid, and quietly ambitious. It should feel like a well-kept laboratory ledger: dense enough for experts, clear enough for a new collaborator, and unwilling to turn activity into accomplishment.

## Anti-references

No generic AI startup landing page, dark terminal costume, celebratory dashboard for failed gates, interchangeable card grid, speculative model leaderboard, or status display that confuses service availability with theorem-proving capability.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before narrative: every headline number exposes its denominator, gate, and source.
2. Failure is a result: nulls, regressions, and blocked runs remain visible.
3. Runnable beats promotional: visitors can execute a real TLA+ check and open the complete notebook.
4. Separate layers of truth: toolchain health, cluster health, model training, and protected evaluation are distinct states.
5. Public context is durable: the page must stand on its own without a Codex session.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, reduced-motion behavior, high text contrast, non-color status labels, and readable layouts from narrow mobile screens through large research displays.
