Evaluation criteria

Acceptance criteria

  1. 01

    Freeze the test. We fix the cases, hashes, budget, and scoring rule before training.

  2. 02

    Check the meaning. A parse is not enough. A candidate must pass non-vacuous TLC and semantic checks.

  3. 03

    Keep failed runs. Rejected, null, partial, and retracted runs stay in the record.

  4. 04

    Separate status from capability. A running job, healthy endpoint, or lower loss is not theorem proving.
