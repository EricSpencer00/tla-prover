The rules behind the numbers

No self-certification.
No moving denominator.
No reward hacking.

  1. 01

    Freeze before training. Protected cases, hashes, and acceptance predicates are fixed before a candidate sees feedback.

  2. 02

    Verify meaning. Syntax is necessary. Non-vacuity, intended properties, and real proof evidence decide acceptance.

  3. 03

    Keep the negatives. Failed branches and rejected edits remain evidence. They prevent cycling and expose local minima.

  4. 04

    Separate status from capability. A healthy endpoint, lower loss, or completed GPU job is never a theorem-proving result.
