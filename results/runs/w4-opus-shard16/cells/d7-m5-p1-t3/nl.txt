A container-ship crane yard maintains a single shared stow-plan record that all gantry cranes must agree on before it changes. Any crane may propose the next revision, and when it does it snapshots the plan's current revision number as the base its proposal builds on. The proposed revision is committed to the shared plan only after a quorum of cranes has voted for it; each crane casts at most one vote per open proposal. When a proposal gathers its quorum it is appended to the plan's revision log, recording which crane proposed it and which base revision it was built on, and the plan's revision number advances by one. If a proposal cannot gather a quorum it is aborted and a fresh proposal may start. Cranes can be slow: a lagging crane may simply not have voted yet, but no crane ever fails outright, so every open proposal can eventually be resolved. The hazard the quorum guards against is a proposal committing on top of a base revision that a different proposal has already superseded, which would silently discard the intervening revision.
SAFETY PROPERTY: Every committed revision in the plan's log was built on the revision that immediately precedes it, so no committed revision is ever silently discarded.
