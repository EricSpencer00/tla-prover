An airport baggage router makes a final, irreversible dispatch decision for each bag: once a bag is sent down its outbound chute it is physically gone and cannot be recalled. Exclusive authority to dispatch is granted by a single token that circulates around a ring of router nodes; the token is passed from each node to its ring successor. A node may finally dispatch a pending bag only while it holds the token, which moves the bag to dispatched and records that a dispatch has occurred for it. A privileged operations administrator holds an override: the administrator may seize the ring at any moment, which suspends token circulation and node dispatching and gives the administrator sole authority; while in control the administrator may itself dispatch a pending bag. The administrator later releases the ring and normal token-driven dispatching resumes. The whole design exists to ensure that the irreversible dispatch of any given bag happens at most one time, whether it is carried out by a token-holding node or by the administrator.
SAFETY PROPERTY: No bag is ever dispatched more than once.
