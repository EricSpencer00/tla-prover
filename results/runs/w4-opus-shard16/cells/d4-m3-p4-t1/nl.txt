A car-wash conveyor line can physically hold only a bounded number of cars at once. A pool of bay controllers runs a leader election: whenever there is no leader, any controller may stand and become the leader for a new term, and the leader can fail, vacating leadership so a failover election chooses another. Only the current leader issues movement tickets. An "enter" ticket asks to bring a new car onto the conveyor and an "exit" ticket asks to drive one off; issued tickets sit in a shared queue. Because the shop-floor network reorders messages, the conveyor may apply any pending ticket in any order, not the order it was issued. Applying an enter ticket brings a car on only if the conveyor still has room; applying an exit ticket drives a car off only if there is a car present. Tickets, once applied, are marked done. The leader may fail and be replaced at any time while tickets remain in flight. No matter how tickets are reordered or how leadership changes hands, the number of cars on the conveyor must never exceed its physical capacity.
SAFETY PROPERTY: The number of cars on the conveyor never exceeds the line's fixed capacity.
