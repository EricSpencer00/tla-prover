A network of vending machines shares one coin vault that only a single machine controller may open at a time; this is the critical resource under mutual exclusion. Access is coordinated by epochs. A machine acquires the vault grant when the grant is free or when the current grant belongs to a retired epoch; acquiring stamps the grant with the current epoch. Only the machine holding a grant stamped with the current epoch may open the vault and be inside it. When it is done it leaves the vault and frees the grant. A machine can crash silently while holding the vault: it stops acting and cannot leave on its own, so the hub reconfigures to a new epoch to recover; reconfiguring retires the old epoch, and as a barrier it forces every machine out of the vault so a fresh, current-epoch grant can be acquired cleanly. A crashed machine may later recover. Across epoch reconfigurations, silent crashes, and recoveries, the vault must never be open to two machines at the same time.
SAFETY PROPERTY: At no reachable state are two distinct machines inside the coin vault at the same time.
