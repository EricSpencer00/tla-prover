A print shop runs a spooler in which several spooler nodes arbitrate access to one shared physical printer, the critical resource. When a print job wants the printer it moves from idle to seeking and asks the spooler nodes for their grants. Each spooler node may grant its support to at most one job at a time; because the shop network reorders messages, grants may be recorded in any order. A job may seize the printer and start printing once it has collected a quorum of grants; the quorum is set high enough that two different jobs can never both hold quorums at the same time, since every node backs at most one job. A node may withdraw its grant from a job that is not yet printing, freeing it to back another. When a job finishes printing it returns to idle and all of its grants are released; a seeking job that gives up aborts and likewise releases its grants. There is deliberately no separate check that the printer is free: exclusivity must follow purely from the quorum discipline. The result must be that the printer is never driven by two jobs simultaneously.
SAFETY PROPERTY: At no reachable state are two distinct jobs printing on the shared printer at the same time.
