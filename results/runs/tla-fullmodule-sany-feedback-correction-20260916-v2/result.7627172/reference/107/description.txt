Several banks share one interbank settlement batch that only one of them may be
posting to at any instant -- the batch ledger is a critical resource under mutual
exclusion. A bank enters the critical section using optimistic locking: it reads
the shared lock register into a private snapshot, and if that snapshot showed the
register free it attempts a compare-and-set that succeeds only while the register
is still free, at which point the bank is in the critical section. If the snapshot
has gone stale it must re-read and retry. On exiting, the bank clears the lock and
records itself as the last bank to post to the shared ledger. Any bank may crash silently at any time; a
crashed bank stops participating. A crashed bank may later reboot to idle, and if
it happened to be holding the lock when it crashed, rebooting also releases that
lock so others can proceed.
SAFETY PROPERTY: At any moment at most one bank is in the critical section, because a bank is in the critical section only when the shared lock register names it.
